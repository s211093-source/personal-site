透過研究量測、資料分析與實驗室學習，我逐漸把對半導體與工程的興趣轉化成更明確的未來方向，也開始思考自己希望投入的科系與研究領域。

Through research measurements, data analysis, and laboratory learning, I gradually transformed my interest in semiconductors and engineering into a clearer future direction, while also thinking more seriously about the academic fields I hope to pursue.

半導體領域的基本探索

Semiconductor Research and Laboratory Learning

在這段特殊經歷中，我接觸到了半導體元件研究的基本流程，內容包含 HEMT、MOSFET 與 GaN 等相關主題，也實際理解元件特性與量測分析之間的連結。

在學習過程中，我接觸了 Id-Vg、Id-Vd 等量測資料的判讀與整理，並透過 Excel 圖表分析元件在不同條件下的表現。這讓我不只是認識公式或理論名稱，而是開始理解研究中如何從實驗數據觀察元件特性。

此外，我也認識了 Positive Bias Stress（PBS）等壓力測試概念，並看到在特定溫度與時間條件下，元件量測結果前後的變化，例如 Vth 的偏移與曲線差異。

In this experience, I was introduced to the basic process of semiconductor device research, including topics related to HEMTs, MOSFETs, and GaN devices, and I began to understand the connection between device characteristics and measurement analysis.

During the learning process, I worked with the interpretation and organization of measurement data such as Id-Vg and Id-Vd, and used Excel charts to analyze device behavior under different conditions. This allowed me to go beyond simply knowing formulas or terminology and begin understanding how researchers observe device properties through experimental data.

I was also introduced to stress-testing concepts such as Positive Bias Stress (PBS), and observed how device behavior changed before and after testing under specific temperature and time conditions, including shifts in Vth and curve variations.

實驗設備的操作練習

Practice in Operating Laboratory Equipment

除了理論與資料分析之外，我也透過實際觀察與操作，逐步熟悉實驗室中的設備使用方式。這些練習讓我理解，研究並不只是閱讀理論，而是需要把知識落實到每一次量測與操作之中。

在設備操作過程中，我也更能感受到研究工作對穩定性、細節與耐心的要求。從準備、量測到確認數據，每個步驟都需要專注與反覆確認，因為量測過程中，哪怕一毫米的誤差，或僅僅是走動產生的輕微震動，都將大幅影響測量結果，這也讓我開始建立更嚴謹的學習態度。

In addition to theory and data analysis, I gradually became familiar with how laboratory equipment is used through direct observation and practical operation. These experiences helped me understand that research is not only about learning concepts, but also about applying knowledge through each measurement and procedure.

During the process of operating the equipment, I gained a much deeper appreciation for how strongly research work depends on stability, attention to detail, and patience. From preparation and measurement to final data verification, every step required focus and repeated checking. Even a probe position shifted by just one millimeter, or a slight vibration caused by someone walking nearby, could significantly affect the measurement results. These experiences have helped me develop more rigorous operating habits and a more careful learning attitude.

教授與學長姊的經驗分享

Experience Sharing from Professors and Senior Students

在特殊經歷中，除了研究與實作本身，來自教授與學長姊的經驗分享也對我產生了很大的影響。透過他們對研究環境、科系學習與未來規劃的說明，我更具體地看見了自己未來可能前進的方向。

這些分享不只提供知識，也讓我開始思考如何把當下的學習與更長遠的目標連結起來。比起只停留在抽象想像，我更能從他們的經驗中理解真實的大學生活與研究訓練是什麼樣子。

Among these special experiences, not only the research and practice themselves but also the experience sharing from professors and senior students had a strong influence on me. Through their explanations of research environments, departmental learning, and future planning, I gained a much clearer picture of the paths I may pursue in the future.

These sharing sessions did not only provide information, but also encouraged me to think about how my current learning can be connected to longer-term goals. Rather than remaining at the level of abstract imagination, I was able to understand more concretely what real university life and research training might be like.

生涯規劃與未來方向

Career Planning and Future Direction

回顧這些特殊經歷，我認為最重要的收穫，是我開始真正理解自己對半導體與工程領域的興趣，並不是停留在課本中的想像，而是能夠透過研究主題、實驗量測與數據分析逐漸深化。

特別是在接觸元件量測、壓力測試、圖表整理與實驗室學習之後，我更清楚自己喜歡的是一種結合理論、觀察與實作的學習方式。這些經歷也讓我更確定，未來希望持續朝電機、半導體與相關研究方向深入發展。

Looking back, the most important gain from these special experiences is that I began to understand that my interest in semiconductors and engineering is not limited to textbook imagination, but can be deepened through research topics, experimental measurement, and data analysis.

In particular, after encountering device measurement, stress testing, chart-based organization, and laboratory learning, I became more certain that I enjoy a way of learning that combines theory, observation, and hands-on work. These experiences also strengthened my determination to continue developing in electrical engineering, semiconductors, and related research fields.
