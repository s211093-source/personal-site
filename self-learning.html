自主學習｜工程實作與學習規劃

Independent Learning in Engineering Practice

自主學習可以從生活中的各個角落發覺，因此只要具備充足的觀察力，時時刻刻都可進行自主學習。

Independent learning can be discovered in every corner of daily life. With sufficient observation, it can take place at any time.

自主學習的五個面向

Five Aspects of My Independent Learning

主題設定

Topic Design

以 Arduino 作為作品基礎，並結合 ChatGPT 輔助，讓 AI 參與我實際動手製作的過程。

Using Arduino as the project foundation and ChatGPT as assistance, I explored how AI could participate in real hands-on making.

學習目標

Learning Goals

我與同學決定以 Arduino 製作簡易手機殼販賣機，結合程式、硬體、雷切外殼與空間配置設計。

My teammate and I aimed to build a simple phone-case vending machine integrating coding, hardware, laser-cut housing, and internal layout design.

進度規劃

Schedule Planning

我們以六週為目標，按週檢視進度，分階段完成研究、設計、製作、測試與成果報告。

We planned the project over six weeks and reviewed progress weekly, moving from research to design, building, testing, and final presentation.

困難解決

Problem Solving

製作過程中的挑戰主要來自與 AI 的溝通隔閡，以及硬體與外殼組裝的反覆調整。

The main challenges came from communicating with AI effectively and repeatedly adjusting the hardware arrangement and housing.

成果展示

Outcome Presentation

我們最終成功達成目標，製作出功能齊全的手機殼販賣機。

In the end, we successfully achieved our goal and built a fully functional phone-case vending machine.

馬祖高中交流活動

Matsu High School Exchange

這是因為老師認為我與組員在製作時態度認真，因此親自指派我們加入臺中二中與馬祖高中的交流計畫。除了在線上會議中向當地學生展示成果與心得之外，我們也獲得了前往馬祖實地踏訪的機會。

經過這段經驗，我不僅在上述各項個人能力上有所提升，也意外體會到了馬祖的風俗民情，成為我高中生活中一段難以忘懷的回憶。

Because our teacher recognized the seriousness and dedication my teammate and I showed during the project, we were selected to join the exchange program between Taichung Second Senior High School and Matsu High School. In addition to presenting our results and reflections in online meetings, we were also given the chance to visit Matsu in person.

Through this experience, I improved many of the abilities mentioned above and also gained a memorable understanding of Matsu’s local culture, making it one of the most unforgettable memories of my high school years.

右圖為我們與馬祖高中學生進行線上交流的過程，由我們的指導老師主持與補充，我們進行成果報告。

The photo on the right also shows our online exchange with students from Matsu High School, during which our advisor hosted the session, added explanations, and guided us as we presented our results report.

主題設定

Topic Design

在完整的自主學習流程中，設立明確的學習主題是非常重要的環節。而我設定的主題，是以 Arduino 為基礎完成作品，同時使用 ChatGPT 輔助，嘗試讓 AI 參與我實際製作作品的過程。

In a complete independent learning process, setting a clear topic is essential. My topic was to build a project based on Arduino while also using ChatGPT as assistance, exploring how AI could take part in my actual making process.

學習目標

Learning Goals

經過一番思考，我與同學決定使用 Arduino 嘗試製作一個簡易的手機殼販賣機。我意識到這將會是巨大的挑戰，除了 Arduino 的程式碼需要不斷請 AI 協助修改之外，還需要使用按鈕、液晶螢幕、麵包板及電線等零件。

除此之外，我們還需透過雷切的方式，以木頭為材料製作出合適的販賣機外殼，並且不斷將內部空間配置調整到最妥善的狀態。

After careful discussion, my teammate and I decided to use Arduino to build a simple phone-case vending machine. I realized this would be a major challenge, since the Arduino code needed repeated AI-assisted revision, and we also had to use buttons, an LCD screen, a breadboard, and wires.

In addition, we needed to create a suitable wooden housing through laser cutting and repeatedly optimize the internal spatial arrangement.

進度規劃

Schedule Planning

規劃完整的學習進度，是學習過程中首要的任務。要確保學習穩健進行、不致中斷，就必須為每個階段設定 deadline。

我們計畫使用六週達成目標，前兩週進行基礎設計以及研究販賣機的運作原理，再以兩週時間進行硬體與程式碼的製作，一週進行測試，最後一週完成成果報告。我們以週為單位，檢視是否有跟上預定進度。

Planning a complete schedule was the first priority of this learning process. To keep the work moving steadily without interruption, each stage needed a clear deadline.

We planned to finish the project in six weeks: two weeks for foundational design and studying how vending machines work, two weeks for hardware and coding, one week for testing, and one final week for the presentation. We reviewed our progress on a weekly basis.

困難解決

Problem Solving

與 AI 的溝通隔閡

Communication Gap with AI

製作過程中，我們遇到了許多困難。其中，與 AI 的溝通隔閡在製作初期最為常見，但後來也逐漸熟能生巧。我們調整了提問方式，以條列式列出遇到的問題與希望加入的功能，這也訓練了我的表達能力。

We encountered many difficulties during the project. In the early stages, the communication gap with AI was especially common, but over time we became more skillful. We adjusted the way we asked questions by listing problems and desired functions clearly, which also strengthened my ability to express ideas.

硬體組裝

Hardware Assembly

至於硬體組裝方面，則是透過不斷試錯，一次次調整硬體擺放位置與外殼形狀之後，才逐一解決問題。這也顯著提升了我的基礎手作能力。

As for hardware assembly, we solved the problems through repeated trial and error, continuously adjusting component placement and the shape of the outer shell. This significantly improved my hands-on making ability.

成果展示

Outcome Presentation

我們最終成功達成了目標，做出了功能齊全的手機殼販賣機。

In the end, we successfully achieved our goal and built a fully functional phone-case vending machine.
