Exploring Engineering Practice and Research

我是一位重視分析、實作與反思的高中生， 希望透過自主學習、專題製作與研究探索， 逐步累積進入工程與半導體相關領域所需的能力與視野。

I am a high school student who values analysis, hands-on practice, and reflection. Through self-directed learning, project work, and research exploration, I aim to gradually build the abilities and perspective needed for future studies in engineering and semiconductor-related fields.

我是個細心又充滿好奇心的學生，對物理、電機與工程相關科系有濃厚興趣， 喜歡探索事物背後的運作原理。 受到在半導體產業工作的父母影響，我從小就對工程領域充滿熱忱，也樂於動手實作， 例如組裝電腦、進行與材料或半導體元件相關的實驗與測試。

在團隊合作中，我重視誠信與責任感，願意主動分工與協調， 也期望未來能把熱情與工程專業結合，在高科技領域持續追求創新並應用於實務。

I am a meticulous and inquisitive student with a strong interest in physics and electronic engineering, and I enjoy exploring how things work from the ground up. Influenced by my parents who work as semiconductor engineers, I have developed a deep enthusiasm for engineering, as well as a love for hands-on learning such as computer assembly and experiments related to materials and semiconductor devices.

I value honesty, responsibility, and teamwork, and I am willing to take initiative in collaboration. In the future, I hope to combine this passion with engineering research, pursuing continuous innovation and practical applications in high-tech fields.
